Overview

Silvara Arboricultural Consultancy provides professional, independent arboricultural advice and reports to a wide range of clients.

We work with private landowners, estates, property managers, developers and commercial organisations, delivering informed and practical solutions to support the responsible management of trees and their surrounding environments.

Our approach combines professional knowledge, detailed assessments and clear, straightforward reporting to help clients make informed decisions, manage risk and protect valuable trees for the future.

Healthy trees. Safer environments. Better decisions.
